## Findings
1. **ADR text, Section 6.2 cost bound (documentation; no code change).** The round-trip bound `6 + 2T + 4W` is below the measured cost: an idle pass 7 round trips; reserving 10 or 50 workers 81 or 360 (about 7 per reservation); revoking and reserving 10 or 50 workers 101 or 460 (about 2 per revoke). The 48 and 408 per second figures derived from it are low. Attempt rate (1/I) is unaffected and pinned. `D` stayed below `I`: at most about 0.28 x `I` at `B` = 50 (one run, one machine, lightly loaded database). Note 35.
2. **Behaviour that differs from the Section 6.1 wording of FS-22.** A late poll that finds the pass heartbeat stale runs the refresh itself, counts the lapse and can claim the job it lapsed (the first test version saw 200). By design (note 3); with the loop running the heartbeat is fresh and the poll gets 204. Note 32.
3. A flaky first version of `TestFault_CancelRacesAccept` (idle workers lost eligibility on a slow run) was fixed in the test (`Keepalive` before each round) before the gates.

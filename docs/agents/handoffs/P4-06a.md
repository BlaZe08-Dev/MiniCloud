## Proposed Disposition for F-4c-b-2 and F-4c-b-3:

- **F-4c-b-2** (Unextended lease during slow cancel delivery races sweeper):
  - *Analysis*: Governed by ADR-0016 Race 2 and `docs/protocol.md` Section 6.1. When cancellation is requested, `/renew` intentionally returns `cancel_requested: true` without extending `lease_expires_at`. If worker result delivery exceeds remaining lease time, the sweeper marks the attempt and job `CANCELLED`. Subsequent delivery receives HTTP 409 `JOB_ALREADY_TERMINAL`, which the worker treats as final and halts.
  - *Proposed Disposition*: Close as intended distributed behavior. Do not register in `findings.md`.
- **F-4c-b-3** (In-flight result upon lease-lost rejection):
  - *Analysis*: Governed by Invariant 3. If a worker has an in-flight result delivery racing lease expiration or sweeper loss, the coordinator evaluates lease and attempt state under row lock and rejects stale results with HTTP 409 `LEASE_EXPIRED` or `STALE_ATTEMPT_REJECTED`. The worker halts without retry.
  - *Proposed Disposition*: Close as intended distributed behavior. Do not register in `findings.md`.
